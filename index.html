Make time for what matters.

Use Tag to connect Actions to the parts of life that matter to you—work, health, learning, and more. Related Actions can share settings and build a record of your progress, so you spend less time setting things up and see where your time goes.

FEATURES

One Action.
Many ways to grow.

Doforia starts with the familiar simplicity of a todo app. It calls tasks and habits Action, connecting the things you do each day with the bigger goals you choose to pursue.

TASKS

From a goal to a plan.

Create a Task or Habit with a date, priority and tags. Break bigger tasks into multiple levels, then use the planned Map Editor to explore the structure as a tree and add sub-Actions.

Product concept
FOCUS TIMER

Focus on what matters.

Attach a Focus Timer to an Action when useful. Arrange focus, rest and Light Work periods. Planned Cycle tracking and the Today Dashboard connect work time and completion records.

Product concept
HABITS

See your consistency.

Follow recurring habits and connect activity to Tags. Review calendars, weekly dots and Streak markers. Set long-term Achievements, like reading hours or habit milestones.

Product concept
WORKSPACE

Make the space yours.

Arrange and resize Modules on Today. Mix text, dates, Sticky Notes and time cards. Customize visual themes, colors, sounds and menus as your workspace evolves.

Product concept
JOURNAL

Remember more than tasks.

Use Diary & Calendar to plan and review the day. Emotional Dustbin is envisioned as a private writing space for thoughts and emotions.

Product concept
ADVANCED TOOLS

Reduce the friction.

Planned desktop Widgets, app and website blocking, automatic tool launching, and conditional blocking based on Action or Tag Cycle goals help create an environment for focused work.
