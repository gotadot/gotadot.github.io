What is Doforia?

Doforia combines the practicality of a to-do list with the euphoria of making meaningful progress. Built as a self-improvement system its own creator would use every day, it helps you move beyond living on autopilot and become more intentional about your life. With less friction and mental effort, it's easier to do what needs to be done—and move closer to the person you want to become.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN / YOURS BY DEFAULT

OWNERSHIP & INDEPENDENCE

Your Doforia.
Always yours.

Doforia is built on a simple belief: your digital life belongs to you.

Create your own independent identity—no Gmail, Google, or Microsoft account required for local use. Your personal workspace runs locally, with your data stored on your device. Our vision is for your identity and workspace to move with you across devices, without depending on a central cloud service.

Plus purchases and Pro subscriptions are tied to your identity, not your email, device, or operating system. You pay for additional capabilities, never for ownership of your own data.

Even if we disappear, your Doforia remains yours.

Freedom comes with responsibility.

Protect your identity keys and recovery backups. If you lose both, we cannot recover your encrypted data for you.

SHARED WORKSPACES / PRODUCT VISION

Your space.
Our space.

Your Personal Workspace belongs to you alone. A shared Workspace is different: a space you choose to build with others.

Start with a blank canvas. Bring company departments together, keep a team project's progress in view, or let friends and loved ones share today's plans and what's coming next.

Different people. One space to move forward together.

Shared collaboration is part of Doforia's planned experience.

FLOATING WIDGETS

Keep your work in sight.

Pop out your Actions, progress, Sticky Notes and other widgets into floating windows. Keep the information you need visible while working in other apps, without constantly switching back to your planner.

CONNECTED ACTIONS

One Action. More progress.

Use Tags to connect Tasks and Habits, so one Action can contribute to related goals. Set a shared daily time budget through a Main Tag: give Work five hours, spend two on one Action, and three remain for the others that follow the preset.

TASKS × HABITS × SHARED TIME BUDGETS
MAP EDITOR

See the bigger picture.

Turn long-term projects into an expandable Action map. Explore branches like a map, then add and arrange components directly in the editor to break large goals into manageable steps.

LONG-TERM PLANNING
FOCUS TIME

Stay in flow. Track the work.

Choose Continuous mode to work without a forced break interrupting your flow. Your time remains part of the work record even when your rhythm isn't a perfect cycle. Prefer structure? Interval mode offers classic Pomodoro-style focus and rest periods.

CONTINUOUS / INTERVAL
FOCUS AUTOMATION

Shape your environment for focus.

Start an Action and Auto Launch opens your chosen work tools. Focus Block places a shield over selected apps and supported website pages during focus, rather than automatically closing your work. Restrictions lift when you pause or finish, and during Interval rest.

ACHIEVEMENTS

An achievement is more than a finish line. Doforia connects milestones to the Actions, time and effort behind them—so you can look back at the process, not just the result.

LOCAL-FIRST / PEER-TO-PEER VISION

Your devices.
One workspace.

Imagine starting an Action on your phone and continuing it on your computer, even across different operating systems. Our goal is peer-to-peer synchronization: your devices communicate directly whenever possible, with no central server required to store your workspace. Your data stays yours, and local work does not depend on an internet connection.

Windows · DemoWebmacOSiOSLinuxAndroidXR

Cross-platform support and peer-to-peer synchronization are planned. Some network conditions may require a connection relay; platform-specific tools depend on device capabilities. The current downloadable Demo supports Windows x64.
